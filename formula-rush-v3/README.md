# Formula Rush 3D V3

Mobile-first WebGL open-wheel racing game.

## V3 changes

- manual throttle: the car remains stopped unless **ACCELERA** is held;
- deterministic vehicle physics with a five-minute simulation stress test;
- steering authority changes with speed;
- bends apply an outward force and require counter-steering;
- stronger braking and realistic arcade caps of about 331 km/h normally and 349 km/h with ERS;
- audio is muted by default and only starts after tapping the speaker button;
- no trees, ramps or pickups on the racing surface;
- road, kerbs, run-off and barriers are generated from the same track spline;
- detailed original procedural open-wheel car with clearcoat paint, halo, suspension, rims and ERS glow;
- Q1 with 22 cars, Q2 with 16, Q3 with 10 and a three-race championship.

## Mobile controls

- hold **ACCELERA** to move;
- use `◀` and `▶` to steer;
- hold **FRENO** before slow corners;
- hold **ERS** together with **ACCELERA** for the boost;
- audio stays off until enabled manually.

The cars use original procedural geometry and team-inspired colours. No official 3D assets or logos are included.
