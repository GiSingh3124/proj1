# Formula Rush 3D V3

Mobile-first WebGL open-wheel racing game.

## V3 fixes

- manual throttle: the car remains stationary until **ACCELERA** is held;
- audio muted by default and enabled only from the sound button;
- no trees, ramps or collectible objects on the racing surface;
- steering authority changes with speed and curves require counter-steering;
- stronger braking and capped top speed: about 331 km/h, 349 km/h with ERS;
- spline-aligned road, kerbs, run-off and barriers;
- more detailed procedural open-wheel cars with halo, suspension, rims and clearcoat materials;
- Q1 with 22 cars, Q2 with 16, Q3 with 10 and a three-race championship.

The 3D cars are original procedural models in team-inspired colours. No official logos or proprietary game assets are included.
